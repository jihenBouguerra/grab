# ✦ Grab

**Paste a social media link → get the video, the audio, or the script.**

Grab is a small self-hosted web app for saving content from Instagram, TikTok,
YouTube, X and most other platforms. Paste a link and choose what you want:
the **video** as an mp4, the **audio** as an mp3, or the **script** — the
spoken words as text you can copy with one tap. Captions are used when the
platform provides them (English, German, Arabic, French); otherwise the audio
is transcribed locally on your machine, so nothing ever leaves your computer.

It runs on your Mac and is made for your phone: open it over your home Wi-Fi,
paste, tap, done. Background jobs with live progress bars keep working even
if your phone locks mid-task.



## Run

```sh
./setup.sh                  # first time only
.venv/bin/python app.py     # then open http://localhost:5001
```
